PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to HEX.COACH. Your privacy is paramount to us. Because our application operates entirely in your frontend browser, we do not require user registration, log-ins, or the submission of personal data to process your cognitive assessments.

1. Information We Collect (or Rather, Don't Collect)

We do not host traditional databases to store your quiz answers, individual power scores, or generated radar charts. Everything runs locally inside your runtime session.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

To keep this server online, we utilize Google AdSense to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Aside from standard third-party ad networks, HEX.COACH does not deploy native tracking cookies to monitor your online identity. Any cookie operations are strictly handled by automated analytical integrations (such as Google Analytics) and ad serving systems.

4. CCPA & GDPR Compliance

If you are browsing from the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, your data rights are naturally protected because we do not hold personal identification records on our infrastructure. For any inquiries regarding infrastructure telemetry, you can review third-party distribution policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this telemetry report configuration at any time. Continued deployment of the network matrix implies validation of these operational terms.
